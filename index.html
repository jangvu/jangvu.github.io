01 / Research

Connecting language, knowledge, and moving images.

My doctoral research explores how structured knowledge can enrich television content—from recognizing entities in noisy transcripts to grounding retrieval and question answering in a large-scale French television knowledge graph.

01

Knowledge Graphs

Construction, semantic representation, and knowledge-grounded access for television archives.

02

Multimodal NER

Entity recognition across speech, text, and video using external knowledge and multi-level ASR.

03

Retrieval & QA

RAG systems that connect natural-language questions with structured multimedia knowledge.

04

Speech & Video

Scalable transcription and understanding pipelines for large TV collections.
